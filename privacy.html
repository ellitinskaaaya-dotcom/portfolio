1. Who processes the data

Questions about your data: el.litinskaaaya@gmail.com.

2. When you just browse the site

The site has no forms, visitor counters or ads, and it doesn’t set cookies. I don’t receive any data about you until you write to me.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses to protect against attacks and processes them under its own rules: docs.github.com. I have no access to these logs.

Fonts are loaded from this same site; no third-party services are used for them.

The site may remember small display settings in your browser (localStorage). They stay on your device and are not sent to me.

3. When you write to me

The “Let’s talk” button and the Telegram, WhatsApp, MAX and Instagram* links lead to messengers and social networks, where their owners’ rules apply. Telegram, WhatsApp and Instagram* belong to foreign companies, MAX to a Russian one.

When you write to me, I receive:

  • your name or username and contact: phone number, username or email;
  • the messages, voice notes and files you send;
  • if we agree to work together — the details needed for the contract and the receipt: for a company or sole trader, its name and tax ID; for an individual, the full name and, if an electronic receipt is needed, a phone number or email.

4. Why and on what legal basis

  • To answer your question, discuss the task and quote a price. Basis: preparing a contract at your request (Federal Law No. 152-FZ “On Personal Data”, art. 6 part 1 clause 5).
  • To do the work and stay in touch about it. Basis: performance of the contract (same clause).
  • To issue a receipt in the “Moi Nalog” (My Tax) app. Basis: a legal obligation (Law No. 152-FZ, art. 6 part 1 clause 2; Federal Law No. 422-FZ, art. 14).

I don’t send newsletters, sell data or use it for advertising.

5. Who I share it with

Only the Federal Tax Service of Russia — receipt details via “Moi Nalog”. Nobody else, unless the law requires it.

I show work done for you, and materials containing your data, in my portfolio only with your consent.

6. Where and for how long I keep it

Messages stay in the messenger you used to write to me. Briefs, contracts and project materials are stored on Yandex Disk, whose servers are located in Russia.

  • If we didn’t work together, I delete the messages and your details one year after the last message.
  • If we did, I keep them for three years after the work ends — the period in which questions about the contract may come up.
  • Receipts are stored in “Moi Nalog” under the tax service’s rules.

7. How I protect it

Only I have access to the devices and accounts where the data is stored.

8. Your rights

You can find out what data I have about you and how I use it, ask me to correct or delete it, and ask me to stop processing it.

Write to el.litinskaaaya@gmail.com or to the messenger where we talked. I’ll reply within 10 working days. If you believe I’m violating your rights, you can contact Roskomnadzor, the Russian data protection authority (rkn.gov.ru), or go to court.

9. Changes

If I change this policy, the new version will appear on this page with a new date. The Russian version of this policy is the main one.
